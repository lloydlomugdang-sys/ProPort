# Official NEU seal

`neu_seal.webp` is the unchanged asset linked as **Official New Era University
Seal** in the university's own About Us page, under "The University Seal":

- Source page: https://neu.edu.ph/main/about-us
- Direct asset: https://neu.edu.ph/main/assets/images/webp/neulogo.webp
- SHA-256: `e3606fcd9814af99d29187bb60fd8a2d4b00e99a724eee0867cad9022554406d`

The seal belongs to New Era University; inclusion does not imply an open-source
license or institutional endorsement of GradPort. Do not redraw or recolor it.
The PDF renderer converts WebP to PNG in memory without resizing, cropping, or
changing the design, embeds it once, and draws that same image at both sides.

Keep this asset available alongside the existing `assets/fonts/` directory in
backend source and compiled deployments. Export never downloads branding assets.
